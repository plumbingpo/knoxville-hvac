Keeping Your Murfreesboro HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and also shows higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is.
