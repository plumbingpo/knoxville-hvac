Full-Service HVAC Care for Chattanooga Homes and Businesses

We service every type of heating and cooling equipment you'll find in local homes and businesses, from central air conditioners and gas furnaces to heat pumps and ductless mini-splits. It makes no difference whether the building is a single-family house, a townhome, or a busy office. Our techs know the local climate, the age and construction of local homes, and the code requirements inspectors enforce, which pays off in every diagnosis.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs tend to be straightforward: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a worn-out blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling its way to an early failure. Tune-ups include coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, and nothing does more to extend the life of your system.

Commercial service makes up a large share of our work as well. We service rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment for restaurant kitchens, retail stores, offices, and multi-unit buildings, and we know that a day without cooling costs the business. To keep that downtime short, we carry common parts on every truck, diagnose fast, and schedule larger jobs around your operating hours whenever possible.

Fast HVAC Response in Chattanooga

If your heat or air quits, how quickly help shows up matters more than anything else. For that reason, we keep our schedule flexible across the area and answer every call with a live person. Emergency calls typically see a technician within a day, and we treat no-heat and no-cool calls as top priority.

The emergency calls we answer tend to follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's warm air from the vents, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil frozen solid. On the heating side, it's a furnace that lights and then shuts down. Year-round, it's breakers that trip every cycle, plugged condensate drains backing water into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have quit. Since our trucks carry capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings, the majority of these calls are wrapped up on the first trip, with the price agreed before any work begins.

A little caution before we arrive can head off a much larger repair. An iced-over AC should be shut off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw faster. Running it frozen risks an overflowing drain pan and a damaged compressor. A short-cycling furnace, or any burning smell, means it's time to kill power at the furnace switch and call us immediately.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Chattanooga

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of the year.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
